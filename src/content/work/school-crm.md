## The task

A Saturday school with 350 students and four branches ran on paper: application forms, exam lists, attendance registers. An application took up to ten days to process, and the director had no single picture of the school.

## What we built

**Admissions in three minutes.** A parent fills in a form online, gets an automatic email and a family number. The office sees the application instantly and moves it through the stages: new → contacted → trial day → enrolled.

**Attendance from the phone.** Teachers mark attendance on a screen built for a corridor: big buttons, allergies and parents' phone numbers at hand.

**One screen for the director.** Branches, groups, finances and compliance in a single view, with exports for the accountant.

## Result

Ten days became three minutes. Four branches work in one system, and the director runs the school from one screen instead of four folders.
