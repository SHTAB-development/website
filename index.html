Marketplace sales · Avito

A CRM that answers customers like a live manager

The seller gets around fifty messages from buyers a day. Answering each one by hand is a full working day of chat. Now the first reply goes out within seconds at any hour: the system holds the conversation like a person, clarifies the details and closes the deal, and the owner steps in only where needed.

  • About 50 conversations a day without the owner
  • Replies in seconds: at night, on weekends, during seasonal peaks
  • Every inquiry stays in the CRM: status, history, reminders

School network · CRM

One system for a school with four branches

A Saturday school: 350 students, four branches, exams and queues of paper forms. An application used to take up to ten days; now a parent files it in three minutes, the office sees it instantly, and teachers mark attendance from their phones.

  • Applications with automatic emails and a number for every family
  • Attendance log: big buttons, allergies and parents' phone numbers at hand
  • Director's dashboard: branches, groups and finances on one screen
